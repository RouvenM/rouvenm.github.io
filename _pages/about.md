I am a postdoctoral researcher in the [Statistical Methods for Big Data group](https://bd.statistik.tu-dortmund.de/) of the Department of Statistics at TU Dortmund University.
I received my PhD (summa cum laude) from Bielefeld University in 2024 with a thesis on *Statistical Inference for Stochastic Process Models in Sports Analytics*.

My research lies at the intersection of statistical modelling and machine learning, specifically hidden Markov models (HMMs) and tree-based methods.

Working with data from various sports (e.g., soccer, American football, handball, and tennis), my goal is to develop models that help evaluate players and teams and support strategic decision-making.
Beyond sports analytics, I am also interested in applications of HMMs to finance, energy, and betting data.
